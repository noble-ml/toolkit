Evaluation

wf.eval(model)                     # architecture: no data needed
wf.eval(model, dataset=val)        # plus accuracy

Without a dataset:

FieldMeaning
parameters, trainablevalues in the model
size_mbfile size of the weights at the model's precision (fp32 4 bytes, fp16 2, int8 1 per value)
flops, macsper image at the evaluated size
latency_ms, fpsbatch 1, forward pass only, measured on the device after warm-up
throughputimages/s at batch_size (default 8)
memory_mbfp32 weights plus one image's activations

With a dataset (a Felix split), additionally:

FieldMeaning
map50, map50_95COCO-style AP (101-point) at IoU 0.5, and averaged over IoU 0.5-0.95
precision_score, recall, f1at the confidence that maximises mean F1 (conf_threshold)
per_class{name: {"ap50", "ap50_95", "instances"}}
confusion_matrix(classes + 1)² counts at confidence 0.25 / IoU 0.45; the last row and column are background

wf.eval prints a table and returns an Evaluation (.show(), .to_dict(), .save("eval.json") or .csv). Options: device, img_size, batch_size, confidence (0.001 for a complete AP curve), iou (0.6), show.

It works before and after training, and never changes weights, BatchNorm statistics or the training state. Passing a whole Felix dataset evaluates its validation split.
