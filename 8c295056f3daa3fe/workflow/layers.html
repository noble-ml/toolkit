Layers

All layers live at wf.<Name>. Shapes are (C, H, W); "in" is the tensor the layer receives. Invalid arguments raise WorkflowError at construction; a bad input shape raises ShapeError naming the stage and operation.

Integer arguments marked *pair* also accept (h, w) tuples.

Conv2D

wf.Conv2D(in_channels, out_channels, kernel_size, stride=1, padding=0, dilation=1)
ParameterTypeDefaultRequirement
in_channelsintrequired≥ 1. Must equal the channels of the tensor it receives.
out_channelsintrequired≥ 1
kernel_sizeint or pairrequiredeach ≥ 1
strideint or pair1each ≥ 1
paddingint or pair0each ≥ 0
dilationint or pair1each ≥ 1

Output: (out_channels, ⌊(H + 2·pad − dil·(k−1) − 1) / stride⌋ + 1, …) (same for W). Errors: wrong input channels; kernel does not fit the input. groups and bias are not exposed: groups is always 1 and bias is automatic (none when the next operation in the same sequence is BatchNorm2D).

BatchNorm2D

wf.BatchNorm2D(channels)
ParameterTypeRequirement
channelsint≥ 1; must equal the input's channels

Output shape = input shape. Uses eps 1e-3 and momentum 0.03. Its running statistics count as non-trainable parameters.

Activations

wf.ReLU(), wf.SiLU(), wf.Sigmoid(): no arguments. wf.LeakyReLU(slope=0.1): slope is the gradient for negative inputs. All accept any shape and keep it.

MaxPool2D / AvgPool2D

wf.MaxPool2D(kernel_size, stride=None, padding=0)
wf.AvgPool2D(kernel_size, stride=None, padding=0)
ParameterTypeDefaultRequirement
kernel_sizeint or pairrequiredeach ≥ 1
strideint or pairkernel_sizeeach ≥ 1
paddingint or pair0each ≥ 0 and at most half of kernel_size

Output: channels unchanged; H and W follow the same window formula as Conv2D. Error: window does not fit the input.

Upsample

wf.Upsample(scale_factor=2)

scale_factor: integer ≥ 1. Nearest-neighbour. Output: (C, H·scale, W·scale).
